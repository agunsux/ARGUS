# TIKUM — SEO & Organic Growth Strategy (2026–2027)

**Brand**: TIKUM (`https://tikum.app`)  
**Parent Company**: SHINERVA HQ  
**Core Thesis**: Dual-Engine Growth Model (Commercial Event SEO + Authoritative Editorial SEO)

---

## 1. The Dual-Engine SEO Philosophy

TIKUM is not an AI content farm nor a generic lifestyle blog. It is an Indonesia-first secondary ticket marketplace anchored in trust, verification, escrow holding, and on-site event assistance.

Our SEO dominance is driven by two interdependent engines:

### Engine 1: Commercial Event SEO (Transaction & Intent Capture)
Captures high-intent buyers searching for specific events, artists, venues, and ticket categories:
```text
Google / Search Engine
        ↓
"Tiket Konser [Event] Jakarta"
        ↓
/events/[slug] or /venues/[slug]
        ↓
Verified Resale Inventory or Primary Ticket Link
        ↓
Escrow Payment / Checkout
```

### Engine 2: Editorial Authority SEO (Topical Trust & Education)
Captures informational search queries about ticket safety, scam prevention, escrow mechanisms, and event guides:
```text
Google / AI Search (GEO)
        ↓
"Apakah beli tiket resale aman?" / "Cara menghindari penipuan calo"
        ↓
/blog/[slug] or /how-it-works / /buyer-protection
        ↓
Trust & Verification Education
        ↓
Contextual Internal Link: /events/[slug]
        ↓
Active Resale Listing & Conversion
```

## 2. Multi-Horizon Implementation Roadmap

### Horizon 1: 0–30 Days (Foundational Health & Trust Crawlability)
* **Objective**: Establish perfect technical crawlability and zero-error indexation.
* **Key Deliverables**:
  * Dynamic XML Sitemap (`/sitemap.xml`) indexing canonical events, venues, cities, trust hubs, and published articles.
  * Enhanced robots.txt blocking faceted search queries (`/*?*q=`) and private surfaces (`/admin`, `/pay`).
  * Dedicated Trust Authority URLs: `/how-it-works`, `/buyer-protection`, `/seller-protection`, `/ticket-verification`, `/escrow`, `/disputes`.
  * Complete Schema.org JSON-LD suite: `Organization`, `WebSite`, `Event`, `BreadcrumbList`, `Place`, `FAQPage`, `Article`.
* **Leading Indicators**:
  * 100% of canonical URLs indexed without crawl errors.
  * Zero structured data warnings in Google Search Console / Rich Results Test.
  * 0% crawl budget wasted on duplicate search parameter queries.

### Horizon 2: 30–60 Days (Venue, City & Category Programmatic Expansion)
* **Objective**: Dominate location-specific and venue-specific searches without creating thin doorway pages.
* **Key Deliverables**:
  * Grounded Venue Landing Hubs (`/venues/[slug]`): GBK, JIExpo Kemayoran, TIM, Indonesia Arena, JIS, ICE BSD, Stadion Siliwangi, Eldorado, Grand City, GBT, Peninsula Island.

  * Category Hubs (`/categories/[slug]`): Konser, Festival, Sepak Bola, Bola Basket, Comedy.
  * Publication of 8 foundational Pillar C (Safety) & Pillar D (Resale Education) articles.
* **Leading Indicators**:
  * Ranking in Top 5 for "Venue [X] jadwal konser".
  * Average CTR $> 4.5\%$ on venue and event search impressions.

### Horizon 3: 60–90 Days (Artist SEO & Topical Authority Cluster)
* **Objective**: Capture artist-specific ticket intent and cement TIKUM as Indonesia's safest ticket resale brand.
* **Key Deliverables**:
  * Artist Discovery Pages (`/artists/[slug]`) for verified touring acts (e.g. Coldplay, Guns N Roses, Ed Sheeran).

### Horizon 4: 90–180 Days (AI / GEO Search Optimization)
* **Objective**: Become the definitive referenced source for ticket safety and event discovery in AI answer engines (ChatGPT Search, Perplexity, Google Gemini, Copilot).
* **Key Deliverables**:

* **Leading Indicators**:

### Horizon 5: 6–12 Months (Regional Scale & Localization)

  * Expansion to Singapore and Southeast Asia regional tour stops.

## 3. Leading Indicators & Success Metrics

We deliberately reject vanity metrics or promising "Page #1 Google". We measure what we control:

1. **Crawl & Indexation Efficiency**:
   * Indexable URL inventory coverage: $> 95\%$ of active canonical events indexed within 48 hours of ingestion.
   * Zero indexation of thin, expired, or empty entity pages.
2. **Topical Authority & Content Quality**:
   * Average Content Quality Engine score $> 85/100$ across all published articles.
   * Internal link density: 3 to 5 verified contextual links per article.
3. **Acquisition & Commercial Conversion**:
   * Organic landing page sessions to Event Detail Page click-through rate ($> 8\%$).
   * Organic visitors initiating ticket creation or buyer checkout.
